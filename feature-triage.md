### Priority 1: Act Now (Core "Aha!" Loop)
*For a Gen Z educational project targeting a viral moment, the app needs to instantly show users money they didn't know they were losing, using impressive modern tech.*

- **SMS/Email Parsing Engine (Local LLMs)**: 
  - *Why*: Deeply technically impressive for an educational project portfolio. Avoids the friction of banking APIs, respects privacy, and auto-builds the subscription graph.
- **The "Shadow Subscriptions" Reveal**: 
  - *Why*: This is the exact viral "Aha!" moment. Showing a Gen Z user 3 forgotten apps costing them ₹1,500/mo creates immediate shock value.
- **The "Subscription Health Score"**: 
  - *Why*: Highly shareable. Gives a quick snapshot of financial bloat that users can screenshot and share.

### Priority 2: Plan Next (The "Decision" Engine)
*Once the graph is built and the user has the "Aha!" moment, how does the app proactively help them?*

- **Actionable Push Alerts**: 
  - *Why*: Proves this isn't just a dashboard. Sending "You haven't used Netflix in 14 days, pause it?" before a renewal is high-value interaction.
- **Price Hike Detector**: 
  - *Why*: Great algorithmic feature to build for an educational project. Tracks historical data and alerts on "stealth" price bumps.

### Priority 3: Collect More Signal (High Barrier to Entry)
*These are powerful, but carry massive technical or regulatory overhead that might stall an educational project.*

- **UPI Autopay Sync**: 
  - *Why*: Requires official banking/NPCI integration, which is notoriously difficult to get access to for an educational project.
- **One-Click Pause (Virtual Cards)**: 
  - *Why*: Requires partnering with a card issuer (like M2P or a bank). Too heavy for V1, though conceptually brilliant.

### Priority 4: Decline or Defer (Mismatched Segment)
*These features conflict slightly with the target audience or duplicate other features.*

- **Subscription Haggler**: 
  - *Why*: Your prompt noted this is for "enterprise or SaaS tools." Our target is Gen Z consumers. Finding discount codes for B2B tools doesn't fit the Gen Z persona.
- **Gamified Savings**: 
  - *Why*: Nice to have, but the "Shadow Reveal" and "Health Score" already provide the necessary psychological hook. Too much gamification can dilute the core message.

### Patterns and Insights
1. **The Tech Flex**: Using on-device Local LLMs for SMS parsing is a phenomenal standout feature for an educational project. It solves the Indian context (heavy SMS usage for transactions) without needing complex server-side data privacy compliance.
2. **The Viral Loop**: Parse SMS -> Reveal Shadow Subscriptions -> Generate Health Score -> User realizes they are losing money -> User shares how much the app found for them.
